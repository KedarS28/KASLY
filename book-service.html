Identity

A valid state ID is required.

Kasly requires a valid, government-issued state ID on file for every customer account. This confirms you meet our 18+ age requirement and keeps bookings tied to a real, verifiable person — for your safety and your Professional's.

Accepted ID
  • State-issued driver's license
  • State-issued ID card
  • U.S. passport

ID is verified securely and never shared with your assigned Professional.

Account & privacy

Deleting your account.

Deletion takes up to 7 days.

You can request account deletion anytime from your account settings or by emailing privacy@kasly.com. To protect against accidental or malicious deletion requests, and to allow time to resolve any active bookings, deletion is processed within 7 days rather than instantly.

Any active or upcoming bookings must be completed or cancelled before deletion finalizes. You'll receive an email confirmation once your account and associated data have been fully removed.

Day 0
Request submitted
Days 1–6
Active bookings resolved
Day 7
Account & data deleted

Ticket & ID numbers

Every booking is assigned a ticket number (e.g. TICKET-1042) and every Kasly Professional an ID number (e.g. PRO-318) — reference these when you call or email support, it's the fastest way for our team to find your record.

When an account is deleted, its ticket/ID number is retired for 6 months before it's eligible to be reassigned to a new user. This gives our team a clean window to resolve any lingering disputes or records tied to that number before it's reused.
